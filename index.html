How to play

Memorize the map for 5 seconds. Then ride a three-lane road: press Left/A or Right/D, swipe, or tap the buttons to change lanes and dodge obstacles. When the timer bar appears, the same controls turn you left or right instead. Traffic drives with and against you, cars cross the road without warning and some are halted with hazard lights. Collect coins for tips, grab power-ups (🛡️ shield, ⏳ chill, 🧲 magnet) and pass cars closely to build a combo that boosts your bonus. The ride gets much faster after every turn, with denser traffic and shorter turn windows. Your bike can take two hits (obstacles or the wall). After a hit it blinks and is protected for a moment, and health recovers after a few seconds. A second hit before it recovers, or a wrong turn, ends the run. You can turn any time until you reach the wall. Finish every turn to deliver and earn the reward for your difficulty (Easy ₹300, Normal ₹500, Hard ₹800); harder levels are faster with more traffic.
